# Key Terms — 2.5 Proteins & Nucleic Acids

The words you need for this lesson, in plain language.

## Protein building blocks

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Protein** | One or more chains of amino acids with a working 3D shape. |
| **Monomer** | One small building block that links with others. |
| **Amino acid** | The building block of proteins. There are about 20 kinds. |
| **Side chain (R-group)** | The part of an amino acid that is different in each one. |
| **Peptide** | A short chain of about 2–50 amino acids. |
| **Polypeptide** | A long chain of about 51 or more amino acids. |
| **Enzyme** | A protein that controls how fast reactions go. |

## Levels of protein structure

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Primary structure** | The order of amino acids in a chain. |
| **Peptide bond** | The strong covalent bond that joins two amino acids. |
| **Dehydration synthesis** | Joining two pieces by giving off water. |
| **Secondary structure** | Small coils and zigzag sheets held by hydrogen bonds. |
| **Hydrogen bond** | A weak pull between atoms *(OpenStax Biology 2e)*. |
| **Tertiary structure** | The whole 3D shape of one chain. |
| **Hydrophobic effect** | Water-fearing amino acids hide in the center when a chain folds. |
| **Quaternary structure** | Two or more chains fitted together. Not all proteins have it. |
| **Subunit** | One polypeptide chain inside a bigger protein. |

## Nucleic acids & DNA

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Nucleic acid** | A molecule that stores and passes on information from parents. |
| **Nucleotide** | The building block of nucleic acids: sugar, phosphate group, and base. |
| **DNA** | A nucleic acid with bases A, T, C, G and the sugar deoxyribose. |
| **RNA** | A nucleic acid with bases A, U, C, G and the sugar ribose. |
| **Uracil (U)** | The base RNA uses in place of thymine (T). |
| **Double helix** | DNA's shape: two strands twisted around each other. |
| **Antiparallel** | DNA's two strands run in opposite directions. |
