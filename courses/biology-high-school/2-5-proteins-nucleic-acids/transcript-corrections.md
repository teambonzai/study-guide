## Things said more clearly

- **Slide 6 notes** first call tertiary structure the 3D arrangement of "polypeptide chains."
  Later, the same notes call it the structure "of a polypeptide." So tertiary structure is the
  shape of **one** chain. The notes say two or more chains together is quaternary.
- **Slide 10** says "Alphabets." It means single letters.
- **Slide 5 notes** say "carbonyl O" and "amino H."
- **Outside source:** Carbonyl O is the oxygen in a C=O group. Amino H is the hydrogen in an N–H
  group. Example: nail polish remover also has a C=O group. *(OpenStax Biology 2e)*
