## Two things said more clearly

- **Slide 6 notes** first call tertiary structure the 3D arrangement of "polypeptide chains."
  Clearer: tertiary structure is the shape of **one** chain, and several chains together is
  quaternary *(Slide 6 notes; OpenStax Biology 2e)*.
- **Slide 2** says a peptide is about 2–50 amino acids and a polypeptide is 51 or more. Other books
  may split them differently, so use your teacher's numbers for this class *(OpenStax Biology 2e)*.
