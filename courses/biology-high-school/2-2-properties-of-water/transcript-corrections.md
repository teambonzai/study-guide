## A few slide lines made clearer

- **Specific heat capacity (Slide 7).** The slide says "of water." But every substance has a specific heat, and water's is high *(OpenStax Biology 2e)*.
- **"Ad" = between (Slide 4).** The prefix "ad" really means "to," so adhesion is water sticking **to** something else *(Merriam-Webster)*.
- **Suspensions "float" (Slide 9).** Big bits like sand don't dissolve, and if you wait, they sink to the bottom *(OpenStax Chemistry 2e)*.

None of this changes what you need to learn.
