## A few slide lines made clearer

- **Specific heat capacity (Slide 7).** The definition says "of water." But the picture also gives specific heats for copper and aluminum. So other materials have one too.
- **Units on Slide 7.** **Outside source:** "/kg" means the numbers are for 1 kilogram of each material. A one-liter bottle of water is about one kilogram. *(NIST)*
- **"Ad" = between (Slide 4).** **Outside source:** In dictionaries, the prefix "ad" means "to." So adhesion is water sticking **to** something else. Tape *adheres*, or sticks, to paper. *(Merriam-Webster)*
- **"Imbalance of forces" (Slide 6).** The picture shows inner molecules pulled in all directions. Surface molecules are pulled inward. **Outside source:** Surface molecules have no water above them, so the pulls are uneven. Think of people at a crowd's edge, pulled only toward the middle. *(OpenStax Chemistry 2e)*
