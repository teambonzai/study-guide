# 2.2 Properties of Water — Lesson Outline

One section per slide, in order. 🎯 = core idea. ⛔ = extra detail.

## Slide 1 — Title ⛔
- 2.2 Properties of Water, part of Unit Two: Biomolecules.

## Slide 2 — The water molecule 🎯
- Water (H₂O) is **polar**.
- Electrons are pulled toward oxygen, so **oxygen is partial negative**.
- The **hydrogens are partial positive**. ⛔ Skip the picture's numbers and model names.

## Slide 3 — Hydrogen bonds 🎯
- A hydrogen on one molecule is pulled to an oxygen on another.
- It is an **intermolecular force** (a force between molecules).
- It happens in other molecules too. It can involve nitrogen, oxygen, or fluorine.

## Slide 4 — Cohesion and adhesion 🎯
- **Cohesion** = water sticking to water. "Co" = together.
- **Adhesion** = water sticking to a different substance. "Ad" = between.
- In a plant, water sticks to itself and to the xylem wall.

## Slide 5 — Capillary action 🎯
- Water rising up a thin tube.
- Caused by **cohesion and adhesion**.
- Thinnest tube = highest water. The curved top is the meniscus.

## Slide 6 — Surface tension 🎯
- The water's surface resists an outside force.
- Caused by **cohesion** only.
- A water strider can stand on the water.

## Slide 7 — Specific heat capacity 🎯
- Energy needed to raise the temperature by 1°C.
- Water warms and cools slowly. This keeps climates and body temperatures steady.
- It helps living things keep **homeostasis**. ⛔ Skip the exact numbers.

## Slide 8 — Hydrophilic, hydrophobic, amphipathic 🎯
- **Hydrophilic** = water-loving, dissolves easily (salts, sugars).
- **Hydrophobic** = water-fearing, doesn't dissolve (oils, fats).
- **Amphipathic** = has both parts (soap bubbles). Builds cell membranes.

## Slide 9 — Suspensions and emulsions 🎯
- **Suspension** = big bits in water that don't dissolve (sand in water).
- **Emulsion** = two liquids that don't normally mix (oil drops in water).

## Slide 9 — Density 🎯
- **Density** = mass per unit volume (D = m / V).
- Water is most dense at **4°C**.
- Ice is less dense than liquid water, so it floats.
