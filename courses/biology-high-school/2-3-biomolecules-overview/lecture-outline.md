# 2.3 Biomolecules Overview — Lesson Outline

The quick-scan version, in slide order. No recording exists. **🎯 = core idea · ⛔ = extra detail.**

---

## 1. Monomers, polymers, and carbon 🎯
- Monomers + monomers = polymer. *(Slide 2)*
- Carbon has four valence electrons, so it forms rings and chains.
- Organic contains carbon. Inorganic does not.

## 2. Energy and the laws of thermodynamics 🎯
- Energy is the ability to change or move matter. It can change form. *(Slide 3)*
- 1st law: energy can't be created or destroyed.
- 2nd law: entropy (disorder) always increases.

## 3. Chemical reactions 🎯
- Chemical reaction: bonds break or form. Melting ice is only a physical change. *(Slide 4)*
- Reactants on the left, products on the right. The coefficient tells how many.
- (s) solid, (l) liquid, (g) gas, (aq) dissolved in water.

## 4. Exothermic and endothermic reactions 🎯
- Exothermic releases heat and can happen on its own. Endothermic absorbs heat and needs energy. *(Slide 5)*
- Energy diagram: compare products to reactants. *(Slide 6)*
- Products lower = exothermic. Products higher = endothermic. ⛔ Activation energy is a later unit. *(Slide 7)*

## 5. Building and breaking molecules 🎯
- Anabolism builds big molecules and uses energy. Catabolism breaks them and releases energy. *(Slide 8)*
- Dehydration synthesis builds by removing water. *(Slide 9)*
- Hydrolysis splits by adding water, like starch into glucose.

## 6. ATP, the cell's energy currency 🎯
- ATP: three phosphates, one ribose sugar, one adenine. *(Slide 10)*
- ATP + water → ADP + Pi + energy. *(Slide 12)*
- ATP is a charged battery. Recharging ADP + Pi takes energy. *(Slide 13)*

## 7. Lipids 🎯
- Made of CHO. Building blocks: fatty acids and glycerol. *(Slide 14)*
- Found in oils, butter, dairy, and waxes. *(Slide 15)*
- Job: long-term energy, 9 kcal/g. Cell membrane, insulation, cell signaling. *(Slide 16)*

## 8. Carbohydrates 🎯
- Made of CHO in a 1:2:1 ratio. Building blocks: monosaccharides. Ending: -ose. *(Slide 18)*
- Found in sugar, bread, pasta, rice, and fruits. *(Slide 19)*
- Job: short-term energy, 4 kcal/g. Cell walls, energy, cell recognition. *(Slide 20)*

## 9. Proteins 🎯
- Made of CHON(S). Building blocks: amino acids. Ending: -ase. *(Slide 22)*
- Found in meat, eggs, beans, tofu, dairy, and nuts. *(Slide 23)*
- Job: backup energy and structure. Enzymes, transport, signaling, immune defense. *(Slide 24)*

## 10. Nucleic acids 🎯
- Made of CHONP. Building blocks: nucleotides. *(Slide 26)*
- Found in any living cells as DNA and RNA. *(Slide 27)*
- Job: genetic information. DNA stores it. RNA helps make proteins. *(Slide 28)*
