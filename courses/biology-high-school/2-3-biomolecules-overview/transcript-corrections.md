## A few clearer versions

These slide ideas are fine to learn as written. Here is a clearer version, so they don't confuse you later.

- **"-ase" for proteins** *(Slide 22)*: -ase really marks enzymes, which are one kind of protein. *(OpenStax Biology 2e)*
- **"Forms cell walls"** *(Slide 20)*: Plant cells have cell walls. Animal cells do not. *(OpenStax Biology 2e)*
- **"Inorganic does not contain carbon"** *(Slide 2)*: A few simple carbon compounds, like carbon dioxide, still count as inorganic. *(OpenStax Chemistry 2e)*
- **Melting ice as "endothermic"** *(Slide 5)*: The ice picture only shows heat flowing in. Melting is still a physical change, not a chemical reaction (Slide 4). *(OpenStax Chemistry 2e)*
